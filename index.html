About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Auburn University. Previously, I was a Term Assistant Professor in Statistics at Columbia University (2019–2022). I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of California, Davis in 2019, advised by Debashis Paul and Alexander Aue.

Education

Ph.D. in Statistics, University of California, Davis, 2019
Advisors: Debashis Paul and Alexander Aue
M.Sc. in Statistics, University of California, Davis, 2014
B.Sc. in Math/Statistics, Shandong University, China, 2013

Research interests

My research interests include high-dimensional statistical inference, Random Matrix Theory and its application, Time Series Analysis, Change-point detection and climate data analysis.
